Hi there!

I'm a Consultant at the World Bank in Washington DC, and an affiliated researcher of the Corvinus University of Budapest Operations and Decision Sciences Institute. Previously, I have been a Managing Economist at Cambridge Econometrics in Brussels and Budapest. I hold Ph.D. and M.A. degrees in Business Economics from Corvinus University of Budapest.

My work focuses on economy-energy-environment (E3) modelling, on business economics and competitiveness. I have taught some courses focusing on these topics.

This page collects materials related to my work. If you are interested in any of them, would like to collaborate or just have a coffee while around DC, please feel free to reach out to me at bence.kiss-dobronyi@uni-corvinus.hu.

You can also visit my Scholar or ORCID profile.
